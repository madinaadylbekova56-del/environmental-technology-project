Environmental Data · Kyrgyzstan

Understanding the problem starts with the data.

Kyrgyzstan faces several environmental challenges, including air pollution, waste accumulation and climate-related changes. Data helps us understand these problems and explore how technology can support environmental monitoring.

01 — AIR QUALITY

Air Pollution

Fine particulate matter is one of the key air-quality concerns in Kyrgyzstan. Monitoring air pollution helps identify patterns and understand where technological solutions could be useful.

02 — WASTE

Waste Management

Waste management requires reliable information about where waste is accumulated, collected and processed. Digital monitoring could make this information easier to use.

58,358.1
thousand tons of Class IV waste present at the beginning of 2025 in Kyrgyzstan

This indicator describes the amount of Class IV waste present at the beginning of the year. It does not represent the amount of household waste generated during 2025.

Sorting

Better information about waste streams can support sorting and recycling systems.
